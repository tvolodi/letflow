| Criterion | Test |
|---|---|
| qa-uat-env: actor-platform-admin resolves to admin-user, admin token found | "resolves to admin-user": output has neither "no seeded user: actor-platform-admin" nor "no valid PLATFORM_ADMIN token", shows `actor-platform-admin@...=OK`, fake source was called with `token admin-user`, token not echoed |

## Review addition (REVIEWER)
The prefix fallback in the per-scenario actors check skips `admin-user`, so an `actor-<x>-admin` with no account of its own reports "no seeded user" instead of silently logging in as the PLATFORM_ADMIN account. Test: "qa-login: an actor-<x>-admin without its own account does NOT fall back to admin-user" (fails on the unfixed script, passes with the fix).
